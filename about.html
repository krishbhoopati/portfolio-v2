I'm a business student who spends a lot of time building things that have very little to do with business school.

I'm studying Business and Computer Science at Wilfrid Laurier University. Right now, I'm working on strategy and technology at Sun Life, scouting startups at Front Row Ventures, and leading StartUp Lab, a student community for people who want to build things.

I've always found it hard to stick to just one thing. I love technology, design, startups, investing, and figuring out how things work. More than anything, I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into something real.

Some things I've done:

  • Built a startup, went through the DMZ, pitched to investors, and raised $10K in grants along the way.
  • Won 7 hackathons and 4 case competitions, building everything from AI-powered tools to platforms for better city planning.
  • Placed 1st out of 300 teams at the TKS x Google Case Competition.
  • Helped bring AI to the workplace at Maple Leaf Foods, building tools and resources supporting 3,000+ employees.
  • Worked on simplifying how teams track projects, collaborate, and make decisions at Sun Life.
  • Joined Front Row Ventures to find and back student founders building ambitious companies.

I've also had the chance to speak at events like Collision Conference, and work with people building things much bigger than themselves.

One thing I've realized is that I enjoy being somewhere between business and engineering. I like understanding why something should exist, just as much as figuring out how to build it.

I think a lot about how much we waste. Time, resources, good ideas, and human potential. I believe technology gives us an opportunity to change that, and I want to spend my life building things that make a difference.

My long-term goal is pretty simple, even if it sounds ridiculous: build something that positively impacts a billion people.

I'm still figuring out exactly what that looks like. In the meantime, I'm trying things, meeting interesting people, and building as much as I can.

I'm usually somewhere between Toronto and Waterloo. If you're working on something interesting, I'd love to hear about it.
