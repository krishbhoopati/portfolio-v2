I've been building things ever since I was a kid, starting with Scratch. At 10, I discovered YouTube tutorials and developed a delusional level of belief that I could learn just about anything.

At 11, I made my first $100 selling lemonade and convinced five of my friends to go door-to-door selling it with me (lol).

I later joined TKS, which completely changed what I thought was possible. I met some incredible people who became lifelong mentors, spent time tinkering with self-driving cars, and worked on autonomous drones at 17.

That eventually led me to the DMZ, where I joined their startup accelerator, pitched at Collision Conference in front of thousands of people, became one of their youngest winners at 18, and secured ~$5K in grants from the 1517 Fund, DMZ, and others. Now I'm on the other side of the table at Front Row Ventures, helping back Canadian student founders with $50K pre-seed cheques.

I started going to hackathons in April 2026 and have won 7 since. That's where I really fell in love with building technology and decided to go all in.

Last summer, I helped bring AI to thousands of employees at Maple Leaf Foods, running workshops and building tools to make it easier to use. Today, I'm at Sun Life, where I've taken an internal product from 0 to 1, owning everything from development and testing, to soon launch. It will be used by 30+ people. This winter, I'll be joining TD as a Business Systems Analyst.
